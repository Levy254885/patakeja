FAQ

Good to know

How do I pay?

You pay with M-Pesa. Once the payment is confirmed you get the landlord's details and a direct link to contact them.

What does the KSh 50 cover?

It unlocks one property's full details and the landlord's contact. It helps keep listings real and saves you from chasing agents.

I am a student. How does Patakeja help?

Tell us which school you attend and we suggest homes that are close, so you spend less on transport and travel time.

I am working. Can I search near my job?

Yes. Add where you work and we suggest locations with a shorter commute.

How much does it cost landlords and agents?

Creating an account costs KSh 100. You can also boost a listing for KSh 500 for 24 hours to get more views.

Do I pay rent through Patakeja?

No. Patakeja helps you find and contact the landlord. You agree rent and deposit directly with them.
